Text description provided by the architects. The need for a major medical program in a place steeped in history is the origin of the design process. Indeed, how to reconcile the scale of the new design with the presence of the Bevaix Castle and Cottage built for the Swiss national exhibition in Geneva in 1896.

The answer is a sculptural project articulated with the existing vegetation and protected buildings. The plasticity of the building is enhanced by the treatment of the openings and the white color.Inside, the spacial quality generated makes the most effective restraint home typology.

Individual units are arranged linearly along corridors, with some sections featuring double-loaded corridors (units on both sides) and others appearing to be single-loaded, potentially opening

The design presents a well-organized, multi-unit complex characterized by its angled massing that embraces a central, semi-enclosed outdoor space. The layout efficiently arranges numerous private units while creating a clear hierarchy between internal circulation and a prominent communal exterior area.

**IV. Design Intent Reading:**

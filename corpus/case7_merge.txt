Text description provided by the architects. This nursing home al Oleiros is a perfect example of what good architecture is: becoming the most restrictive starting conditions into the supporting axis of its development. The programme of this home with 140 places is accommodated across 4991m2 in four floors plus a great covered terrace, which provides a private, outdoor place. Ground floor includes a 62 places-parking and General Services (Laundry, Kitchen, Warehouse and Changing Room Facilities). This level is the building’s base. Its architectural value is its discontinuous and porous design instead massive. This remains “hórreos”, typical Galician architecture. Next to the hall Entrance, a beautiful promenade over the ground floor begins. It is softly articulated by several light entries. Distribution is clear and legible from the route’s start, which allows a safety and comfortable navigation throughout the Units. This issue becomes extremely important in and elderly home. Three autonomous Living-Units grow from the base, connected by light walkways trough communal areas. These Units are designed in accordance with the same scheme: public-filter-private areas. Coliving room, kitchenette and visit room are placed at public zone; filter contains the main corridor and vertical core and, private area is formed by individual and double bedrooms, finally.The crossable green roof is intended for recreative, private use as well as perfect place to spend time with the loved ones. This fifth façade gains depth because of the lower-level views, with green and wooded surfaces. This Elderly House demonstrates its complete architectural quality by getting an Excellence Breeam score. It is important to remark the strong adaptability that Coliving-Units offers. The design is able to ensure not only fire compartmentalization, but also isolating areas in case of disease requirements. A perfect connection between form and function goes through the whole project.

**II. Spatial Relationship Descriptions:**
The design features a predominantly linear organization, with residential units arranged along central corridors or "Galerías." These long blocks of units are interspersed with common areas such as living rooms and dining rooms, which often terminate the blocks or are strategically placed to serve clusters of units. A distinct wing on the right side of the plan houses administrative, medical, and social services, forming a more public and functional zone that is connected to the residential areas. Multiple stairwells provide vertical circulation and emergency egress, breaking up the linear massing. Many common areas and some individual units have direct access to or views of "Relleno Tierra Vegetal" (planted areas), integrating green spaces into the immediate environment. The overall layout suggests a clear separation between private living spaces, semi-public communal areas, and dedicated service zones, all linked by a coherent circulation system.

**III. Overall Spatial Impression:**
The building presents as a comprehensive care facility or residential complex, characterized by its elongated form and a clear functional zoning. It efficiently organizes numerous private living units alongside diverse communal and essential service areas, emphasizing accessibility and a connection to outdoor green spaces.

**IV. Design Intent Reading:**
The design communicates an intent to create a highly functional and supportive environment, likely for elderly care or assisted living. The linear arrangement of units maximizes density and facilitates staff supervision and access, while the integration of dedicated medical, administrative, and social services within the same complex underscores a holistic care approach. The strategic placement of communal living and dining areas, along with the emphasis on direct access to planted outdoor spaces, suggests a focus on fostering community, promoting well-being, and providing residents with opportunities for social interaction and connection with nature. The building's form also appears to adapt to the site's contours, indicating a thoughtful response to its specific context.
